# Current empirical abstract — working draft

Updated 2026-10-02. This is a candidate for a joint paper, not a submission-ready paper or a claim of completed independent human validation. The [historical protocol abstract](abstract.md) is retained unchanged.

Role prompting and actual access to external evidence are distinct interventions in LLM multi-agent systems. We study Role Diversity, actual Information Access Diversity, and Observable Evidence Lineage, using an Isolated Agent Runtime as auditable infrastructure for experimental treatment integrity. Two comparisons were conducted with Qwen3:14b. Study 1 evaluated five conditions on 30 fixed, public-length- and structure-selected HotpotQA distractor development questions, completing 150 unique cells and 510 successful native generations with one repetition. On the pre-specified 24 questions not observed in the preceding pilot, the mean Answer F1 difference between C3 (neutral roles, partitioned documents) and C2 (diverse roles, full access) was −0.1564. Because this contrast changes both role and access, it does not isolate the causal effect of information partitioning. Citation statistics and preliminary saved-output inspection motivated a hypothesis concerning public expression, information retention, and output support. Study 2 fixed the existing C3 worker outputs and compared output-only input, added cited source excerpts, and added token-length-matched neutral text. All 9 smoke and 72 main native responses completed. On the 24 previously observed main questions, Answer F1 was 0.5666, 0.5805, and 0.5805, respectively. The mean difference was +0.0139 against output-only input and zero against the neutral-text control. These observations did not support an excerpt-specific mean answer improvement in this sample; they neither establish the mechanism nor refute information loss generally. A two-pass reference registry frozen before output disclosure and independent stage-wise human Evidence Lineage analysis are planned, not completed. Limitations include a small selected sample, one model and repetition, unequal total computation, an exploratory follow-up on previously observed questions, and pending independent human review. We make no population-wide HotpotQA or general LLM claim.
